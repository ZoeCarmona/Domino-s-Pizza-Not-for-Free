Welcome to Pizza Path

Pizza Path lets our customers enter their address, see the fastest delivery route on a map, and get a clear, real-time estimate of how long their pizza will take to arrive.
Also, it’s a great way for our delivery drivers to check their assigned stops and see the fastest routes to deliver pizzas right on time, with no guesswork or delays.

It’s so simple to use: just enter your destination and the map will automatically trace the fastest route, highlighting the path and displaying the estimated travel time.
